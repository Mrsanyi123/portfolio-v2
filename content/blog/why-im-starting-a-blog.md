I have been writing in public for a while — LinkedIn posts, X threads, YouTube scripts. Those formats are good at one thing: they move fast.

They are bad at another thing. The useful parts disappear. A post that took a week of thinking lives for forty-eight hours, then the algorithm buries it.

This blog is the slower version of that.

## What I want this to be

Not a newsletter. Not a personal brand machine. Just a public notebook.

I will write about shipping products, being a student and a founder at the same time, and the unglamorous parts of building from Ethiopia. Some posts will be practical. Some will just be me figuring something out in writing.

If a thought is still useful six months later, it belongs here.

## What it will not be

I am not going to publish every week on a calendar. I already make enough content. The bar is simple: if I would want to reread it later, I hit publish.

No growth hacks. No "10 tools I used this week." If you want that, the internet already has it.

## Why now

I am sixteen. I am building [Shiplog](https://sanyi.dev/projects) and [YScroll](https://yscroll.xyz), making videos, and still going to school. The year is loud.

Writing is how I slow it down. If you are building something too, I hope a few of these notes save you a month I already spent.
